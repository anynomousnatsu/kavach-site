Common
Questions

What restaurant owners, hotel managers and warehouse supervisors in Kathmandu ask us before they sign.

Ask us something else

How much does pest control cost in Kathmandu?

Restaurant and commercial kitchen programmes start at NPR 4,000 a month on a 12-month contract, with one or two visits a month, depending on pest activity. Large or multi-kitchen sites are quoted. Hotel, warehouse and home programmes, and one-off treatments, are quoted for your site. Send your facility type, floor area and location. We reply within one business day.

Do you treat restaurants during business hours?

No. Kitchen visits are booked in a night window between 10 PM and 5 AM, after you close, at no extra charge. We agree the visit time with you in advance.

What pests do you handle?

Cockroaches, rodents, termites, bed bugs, mosquitoes, drain and house flies, stored-product insects, birds, and snakes or other wildlife that need relocating. We do not offer fumigation.

Will your service help with a DFTQC food inspection?

Pest control is one of the hygiene points food inspectors look at. We leave a written record of every visit, covering what we found, what we did and which products we used, so you can show it when asked. A Kavach service record or certificate is not a government approval. It cannot guarantee the result of an inspection.

What happens if pests come back after a treatment?

If pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service, tell us and we return to re-treat at no charge. This is written into every contract. A guest room found infested during an inspection is a new job, not a callback, and is treated at a separate per-room fee.

Which areas of Kathmandu do you cover?

The whole Kathmandu Valley, including Thamel, Lazimpat, Jhamsikhel, Chabahil, Baneshwor, Balaju, Patan and Bhaktapur. Sites outside Ring Road carry a travel charge, which we tell you before booking.

What products do you use, and what precautions do you take?

In kitchens we use gel baits and targeted crack-and-crevice treatments, applied away from food-contact surfaces, and rodent bait goes inside bait stations. Your service record lists each product, its batch number and when the treated area can be used again. A written method statement is available on request. If young children, pets or anyone with asthma will be on site, tell us before the visit.

Do you do one-off treatments, or only contracts?

Both. Contracts are the core of the business, but one-off treatments for bed bugs, cockroaches, termites, mosquitoes, birds and more are available, quoted on request.
