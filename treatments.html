Treatments & prices

  1. 01

    Bed bug eradication — Gold

    NPR 6,000 (1 BR) → 18,000 (5 BR)

    Three visits: steam + residual, then day-10 and day-20 follow-ups timed to the egg cycle. Steam kills eggs — spraying alone does not, which is why the cheap job comes back. Guaranteed in writing. Most clients move to a monthly programme after their first treatment. It costs less per visit.

  2. 02

    Cockroach one-time gel treatment

    NPR 5,000 (1 BHK) → 9,000 (5 BHK)

    Gel baiting of every harbourage point plus crack-and-crevice residual and monitor placement, so you can see it worked. Most clients move to a monthly programme after their first treatment. It costs less per visit.

  3. 03

    Termite — post-construction

    NPR 20–25 / sq ft

    Drill-and-inject perimeter barrier for standing buildings. Best done in the dry season. Most clients move to an annual inspection programme after treatment. It costs less per visit.

  4. 04

    Termite — pre-construction

    NPR 200–450 / sq m

    Foundation treatment before the slab is poured — the cheapest termite protection a building will ever get. We work through contractors and architects; ask us for a spec sheet.

  5. 05

    Rodent proofing

    Materials + 60% + labour

    Stainless mesh, door sweeps, drain grates and gap sealing. The only pest work that permanently reduces the problem — it's why we recommend it inside every rodent contract, even though it cuts our own callback revenue.

  6. 06

    Mosquito / dengue fogging

    NPR 450 / room indoor · NPR 4,000+ outdoor

    Seasonal (June–September), plus weddings, events, party palaces and schools. Indoor ULV and outdoor thermal fogging, scheduled to avoid your guests and neighbours. Event venues: ask about a monsoon-season standing arrangement. It costs less per visit.

  7. 07

    Insect light traps — supply + service

    NPR 12,000–18,000 / unit + NPR 800 / quarter

    Unit supplied and installed, glue boards replaced and counted quarterly. The count goes into your trend report — it's monitoring, not decoration.

  8. 08

    Bird netting & spikes

    Net NPR 90–250 / sq ft · spikes NPR 600–1,000 / pc · install NPR 85 / running ft

    Hotels, apartments, temples and hospitals. Pigeon-proofing rooflines, ledges and shaft openings with UV-stabilised netting. Hotels: bundle with Room Shield on one account. It costs less per visit.

  9. 09

    Disinfection / sanitisation

    NPR 2 / sq ft

    Schools, clinics and offices. Surface disinfection with hospital-grade products, documented like everything else we do.

  10. 10

    Snake & wildlife removal

    NPR 3,000–6,000

    Humane capture and relocation. Low volume, but yes — we answer the phone at night, and yes, the neighbours will talk about it.

The honest arithmetic
