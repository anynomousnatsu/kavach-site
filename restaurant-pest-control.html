[K-01] Kitchen Shield

Pest Control For
Restaurants & Commercial Kitchens

A recurring programme for restaurants, cafés, bakeries and cloud kitchens across the Kathmandu Valley. One or two visits a month, booked between 5 AM and 9 PM, with a written record of every visit. From NPR 4,000 a month on a 12-month contract.

What Your Kitchen
Programme Covers

  • Cockroach gel baiting at harbourage points: under counters, around motors, in cracks and voids
  • Targeted crack-and-crevice treatment, applied away from food-contact surfaces
  • Drain treatment for drain flies and small flies
  • Numbered, mapped rodent bait stations, each checked every visit
  • Insect light trap glue-board checks and replacement
  • Pheromone monitoring traps to show where activity is building
  • 1–2 visits a month, depending on pest activity

If your kitchen needs new equipment such as insect light traps, or proofing work, we quote it before doing it.

How A Visit
Fits Your Kitchen

  1. 01

    Book a regular slot

    We agree a visit time between 5 AM and 9 PM, for example early morning before you open.

  2. 02

    Prepare the areas

    Before the first visit we tell you what to clear, cover or put away in the areas we will treat.

  3. 03

    Inspect and treat

    The technician inspects, treats harbourage points and drains, and checks every numbered bait station.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    We write the visit up in your logbook and send a photo report on WhatsApp, including when treated areas can be used again.

Kitchen
Questions

What restaurant owners and kitchen managers ask before they sign.

How much does a monthly restaurant pest control programme cost?

From NPR 4,000 a month on a 12-month contract for a standard restaurant, café or cloud kitchen, with one or two visits a month, depending on pest activity. Large or multi-kitchen sites are quoted. Any travel charge or extra work is agreed before booking.

Can you visit before we open?

We work between 5 AM and 9 PM, so an early-morning visit before you open is possible. We agree a regular slot with you.

What should we prepare before a visit?

Before your first visit we tell you what to clear, cover or put away in the areas we will treat. Your record after each visit says when those areas can be used again.

What records do we receive after each visit?

A logbook entry that stays on site, a photo report on WhatsApp, and a Kavach service certificate. A method statement listing products and batch numbers is available on request. A Kavach service record or certificate is not a government approval.

What happens if pests are still seen between visits?

If pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service, tell us and we return to re-treat at no charge. This is written into every contract. Between visits you can message us on WhatsApp at any time; we reply within 2–3 hours, between 5 AM and 9 PM.
