[MOD-04] About

Kavach — कवच — means shield.

We chose the name because that is the entire promise: a permanent, documented barrier between your business and the pest pressure that Kathmandu's climate, drainage and density will never stop producing.

[01] Why We Exist

The law changed. Most operators didn't.

The Food Hygiene and Quality Act, 2081 (2024) now requires every DFTQC-licensed food business to have pest control. Most Kathmandu operators still spray and leave. They hand over no record. That is the gap we were built to close — an auditable, evidence-based service that a health inspector, a corporate procurement lead, or a foreign tour operator can actually verify.

[02] How We Work

Four operating rules.

  1. Night service window.

    Commercial kitchens are treated between 10 PM and 5 AM. Your restaurant does not lose a single cover.

  2. Recurring, not one-off.

    Every client is on a contract. Route density is how we keep prices fixed and quality consistent.

  3. Documentation is the product.

    The wall certificate, the on-site logbook and the monthly photo report are what you're actually paying for.

  4. Callbacks are free.

    48-hour unlimited callback is in every contract. We eat the cost so you don't have to negotiate the emergency.

[03] What We Don't Do

We are pest management specialists. That is all we are.

We do not sell cleaning services, we do not chase government tenders, and we do not do fumigation — that requires specialised licensing which we concede to the incumbents.

Want the evidence file for your premises?

Tell us your facility type and square footage. We publish a fixed price the same day.
