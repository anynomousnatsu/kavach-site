Cockroach Control
For Your Premises

Kavach provides quoted cockroach treatment in Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur, with an ongoing programme for commercial kitchens. Share the affected areas and your operating hours so the scope can be agreed before booking.

Which cockroach service fits your premises?

Your need Service route How it is priced
An active cockroach problem One-off treatment, quoted for the affected site Area, activity and access determine the quote
Ongoing restaurant or commercial-kitchen monitoring Kitchen Shield programme From NPR 4,000/month on a 12-month contract; 1–2 visits a month
A hotel kitchen Kitchen programme quoted separately from guest-room inspections Kitchen scope agreed before booking

A one-off cockroach treatment is not the same purchase as a recurring kitchen contract. Tell us whether you need an assessment of a current problem or regular monitoring.

What happens during a cockroach visit?

  1. Share the site details. Send the facility type, floor area, city and where activity has been seen.
  2. Agree scope and preparation. We explain what to clear, cover or put away before the visit. Service is booked between 5 AM and 9 PM.
  3. Inspect and treat. In kitchens, our confirmed methods include gel baiting and targeted crack-and-crevice treatment away from food-contact surfaces. The agreed treatment depends on what is found.
  4. Receive the record. Your record describes what we found, products and batches used, and when treated areas can be used again.

Do not assume that every product or treatment has the same precautions. Tell us about children, pets or sensitive occupants before the visit so preparation can be agreed.

Cockroach treatment questions

Do you cover Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur?

Yes. Kavach visits customer premises across these three cities. There is no walk-in office. Any travel charge outside Ring Road is discussed before booking.

What is the price of a one-off cockroach treatment?

One-off work is quoted for your site. The published NPR 4,000/month starting price belongs to the 12-month restaurant programme, not a single treatment.

Can you come before a restaurant opens?

A visit can be booked between 5 AM and 9 PM at an agreed time, including early morning where a slot is available. A WhatsApp reply is not a booked treatment.

What happens if pests return?

If pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service, tell us and we return to re-treat at no charge. This is written into every contract. See the callback terms.
