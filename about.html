[MOD-04] About

Kavach Means
Shield

We chose the name because it is the promise: a documented barrier between your business and the pests that Kathmandu's climate, drainage and density keep producing. That is what evidence-based pest control means here.

Why We Write
Everything Down

A pest control visit is only useful if you can show what was done. We built Kavach around that: every visit leaves a written record that a health inspector, a procurement lead or a tour operator can read.

Evidence, not assurances

Every product batch logged, every bait station numbered, every visit photographed.

Records you can show

A logbook on site, a WhatsApp photo report and a Kavach service certificate. A Kavach service record or certificate is not a government approval.

Visits that fit your hours

Booked between 5 AM and 9 PM at a time that suits you, for example before a kitchen opens.

[02] How We Work

Four Operating Rules

  1. 01

    Visits at a time that suits you

    We work between 5 AM and 9 PM and agree each visit time with you in advance.

  2. 02

    Contracts first

    Most of our work is on contracts, because regular visits keep pests down and keep prices predictable. One-off treatments are available too.

  3. 03

    Documentation is the product

    The logbook, the photo report and the service certificate are what you are paying for.

  4. 04

    Callbacks are free

    If pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service, tell us and we return to re-treat at no charge. This is written into every contract.

What We Don't Do

We are pest management specialists. We do not sell general cleaning, we do not bid for government tenders, we do not offer fumigation, and we do not service cold stores at the moment.
