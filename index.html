06 / Questions

Asked in every kitchen doorway

Do you really service kitchens at night?

Yes. Our standard service window is 22:00–05:00, every night, at no extra charge. Your kitchen closes, we arrive, we finish, you open on time. You never lose a single cover.

Are the chemicals safe around food?

We use only pesticides registered in Nepal, purchased from licensed importers. Food-contact zones get gel baits and non-repellent crack-and-crevice applications, never broadcast sprays. Every product, batch number and re-entry interval is logged in your on-site logbook.

How much does it cost?

The full rate card is on our pricing page — monthly programmes from NPR 2,500/month, home plans from NPR 7,000/year, and every one-off treatment listed. No "price after inspection" games; the inspection is still free, but you'll know the number before we knock.

What is the 48-hour callback guarantee?

If pest activity appears between scheduled visits, we return within 48 hours, free, unlimited times. It's written into your contract, not implied on a leaflet.

Does the law require my restaurant to have pest control?

The Food Hygiene and Quality Act 2081 requires pest-control measures at every licensed food premises, and DFTQC inspections check for them. Read our plain-language guide.

Do you do one-time treatments or only contracts?

Both. One-off treatments for bed bugs, cockroaches, termites, rodents and more are published with prices. Most clients move to a monthly programme after their first treatment — it costs less per visit.
