Ask us something else

How much does pest control cost in Kathmandu?

We publish fixed rates instead of quoting after a site visit. Commercial kitchens start at NPR 2,500 a month, hotels at NPR 180 per room per quarter, warehouses at NPR 6,000 a month, and residential AMC at NPR 7,000 a year. Send your facility type and square footage and you get a firm price the same day.

Do you treat restaurants during business hours?

No. Commercial kitchens are treated in a night service window between 10 PM and 5 AM at no extra charge, so you do not lose a single cover. A cold, empty kitchen also shows the harbourage points a working one hides.

What pests do you handle?

Cockroaches, rodents, termites, bed bugs, mosquitoes, drain and house flies, stored-product insects, birds, and snakes or wildlife needing humane relocation. We do not do licensed fumigation. That we concede to the incumbents.

Do I need pest control to pass a DFTQC inspection?

Yes. The Food Hygiene and Quality Act, 2081 (2024) requires every DFTQC-licensed food business in Nepal to have pest control in place. We supply exactly what an inspector asks for: a framed wall certificate, an on-site bound logbook, and a monthly photo report.

What happens if pests come back after a treatment?

We return in the next scheduled window at no cost. The 48-hour free callback is unlimited and written into every contract we sign, from Home Shield through to XL Kitchen. No callout fee, no paperwork, no negotiation.

Which areas of Kathmandu do you cover?

The whole Kathmandu Valley, including Thamel, Lazimpat, Jhamsikhel, Chabahil, Baneshwor, Balaju, Patan and Bhaktapur. Sites outside Ring Road carry a travel charge, quoted up front.

Are the chemicals safe around food, children and pets?

Yes. Commercial kitchens get food-safe crack-and-crevice and gel formulations applied away from food-contact surfaces, and residential work uses kid and pet-safe products. A written method statement listing every chemical and its batch number is provided on request.

Do you do one-off treatments, or only contracts?

Both. Recurring contracts are the core of the business because route density is what keeps prices fixed, but one-off bed bug, cockroach, termite, fogging and bird-netting jobs are all available at published rates.
