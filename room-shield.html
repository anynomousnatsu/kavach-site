[SEC-01] The Reality

How bed bugs actually work

  1. 01

    They arrive in luggage

    Not through dirt or bad housekeeping. A single gravid female in a backpack from any city in the world starts an infestation. The cleanest guesthouse in Thamel is one check-in away.

  2. 02

    They spread room to room

    Through wall voids, conduit runs and corridor skirtings — roughly a room a week once established. By the time a guest complains, adjacent rooms are usually seeded.

  3. 03

    Spraying alone fails

    Sprays don't reliably kill eggs, which hatch in 6–10 days into a "treated" room. That's why the cheap spray job comes back — and why our protocol leads with steam, which kills every life stage on contact.

[SEC-03] The Protocol

When a room is positive

  1. 01

    Steam, same day

    Industrial steam to mattress seams, headboard, frame, skirting and upholstery. Kills adults, nymphs and — critically — eggs on contact. Most Kathmandu operators only spray; steam is our differentiator.

  2. 02

    Residual application

    Targeted residual to cracks, voids and harbourages the steam can't hold — intercepts anything that walks in from an adjacent room.

  3. 03

    Day-10 follow-up

    Timed to the egg hatch cycle. Any survivors from late-hatching eggs are dealt with before they can re-establish.

  4. 04

    Day-20 verification

    Final inspection with monitors. The room goes back on the record as verified clear — and adjacent rooms are inspected at every step.
