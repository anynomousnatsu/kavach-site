Bed Bug Treatment
For Hotels & Homes

Kavach assesses and treats bed-bug problems in Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. Hotel room treatment is quoted separately from the quarterly inspection programme, with the scope and room-use precautions agreed before work starts.

Treatment or regular hotel inspections?

An infested room needs a treatment quote. Kavach's confirmed bed-bug method for guest rooms is steam plus a residual treatment over three visits, with a separately quoted per-room fee. We inspect the reported room and nearby rooms to assess the extent before agreeing the work.

Room Shield is a different service: quarterly inspections and a record card for each guest room. An infestation found during an inspection is a new treatment job, not an included callback. The hotel kitchen is quoted separately.

Compare hotel inspections Quote and callback terms

What should a hotel manager send?

  • Property type and city: Kathmandu, Lalitpur or Bhaktapur.
  • Total room count, suspected affected rooms and where activity was observed.
  • Whether you want a current room problem assessed, regular inspections, or both.
  • Housekeeping and occupancy constraints that affect access.

Do not send guest names or private guest information. Room numbers and relevant observations are enough to begin a discussion.

We agree what to prepare before attendance. A room being treated stays out of use until we advise that it can be used again; timing depends on the treatment, so there is no single reopening time for every room.

Bed-bug treatment questions

How much does bed-bug treatment cost?

Hotel guest-room treatment is quoted per room before work starts. The quote distinguishes inspection, treatment and any minimum or travel charge. No fixed bed-bug treatment price is published.

Is the inspection programme enough to prevent every case?

No. An inspection records what was found that day; it cannot prevent a guest bringing bed bugs into a property later.

Do you treat homes as well as hotels?

One-off bed-bug treatments are available for homes, quoted for the property. Tell us the property type so the assessment and treatment scope can be agreed; do not assume the hotel room arrangement is a household price.

How quickly will you reply?

WhatsApp replies are within 2–3 hours, between 5 AM and 9 PM. We then agree an inspection time. A reply does not promise immediate attendance.

Is a newly affected hotel room a free callback?

A room newly found infested is a separate treatment job. If pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service, tell us and we return to re-treat at no charge, under the written callback terms.

Tell us about your premises

Send the city, total room count and suspected affected rooms. Tell us whether you need inspection, treatment or both.
