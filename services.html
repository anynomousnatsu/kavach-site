Pest Control Services
In Kathmandu

Four programmes for restaurants, hotels, dry warehouses and homes across the Kathmandu Valley, plus ten one-off treatments. Contracts are the core of the business; one-off jobs are available too.

[K-01] Monthly · 12-month contract KITCHEN SHIELD

Kitchen Shield

Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, cloud kitchens, hotel kitchens

from NPR 4,000 / month
  • Cockroach gel baiting at harbourage points
  • Targeted crack-and-crevice treatment, away from food-contact surfaces
  • Drain treatment for drain flies and small flies
  • Numbered, mapped rodent bait stations, each checked every visit
  • Insect light trap glue-board checks and replacement
  • Pheromone monitoring trap readings
  • Logbook entry and WhatsApp photo report after every visit, plus a Kavach service certificate
  • 1–2 visits a month, booked between 5 AM and 9 PM
  • Free return visit if pests come back within 48 hours of a scheduled service

[R-02] Quarterly inspections ROOM SHIELD

Room Shield

Hotels, guesthouses, lodges, hostels

Quoted per property
  • Quarterly inspection of every guest room: seams, headboards, frames, skirting
  • Per-room record card showing the inspection date and findings
  • Rooms found with bed bugs treated with steam plus a residual treatment, at a separate per-room fee
  • Corridors, storage, laundry and back-of-house areas included in inspections
  • Rodent programme for service areas and roof lines
  • Guest complaints: WhatsApp reply within 2–3 hours, 5 AM to 9 PM

Store Shield
