## [0.1.0.1] - 2026-10-12

### Fixed

- Give assistive readers a consistent service/footer heading outline without changing visual heading styles.

## [0.1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added

- Read commercial service information and request quotes in Nepali, with direct language navigation.
- Compare one-off cockroach and bed-bug treatment with recurring kitchen and hotel programmes.
- Use practical buyer guides for restaurant records, hotel assessments and dry-warehouse monitoring.
- Include city, request type and a broad enquiry-source clue in WhatsApp quote requests.
- Choose optional analytics consent when a real account is configured; GA4 remains disabled by default.

### Changed

- Clarified commercial service coverage, pricing, records and exclusions across Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur.
- Added accurate language/page metadata, contextual service links and the expanded sitemap.
- Reduced delivery overhead with licensed local fonts, combined CSS and responsive gallery images.

### Fixed

- Preserved quote prefill in the Nepali form, improved caption contrast and corrected heading order.
- Corrected desktop-header overflow and kept main content readable without JavaScript.
- Clarified that the legacy confirmation page cannot prove a WhatsApp message was received.

### Removed

- Retired FAQ rich-result markup; useful visible FAQ answers remain.
