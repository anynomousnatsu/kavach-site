03 / Safety

The unglamorous part we take most seriously

  1. 01

    PPE & re-entry protocols

    Respirators, coveralls and gloves on every job. Re-entry intervals stated in writing before treatment and never shortened to please a client in a hurry. Not once.

  2. 02

    Food-safe products in food zones

    Food-contact areas get gel baits and non-repellent crack-and-crevice applications only. Broadcast spraying near food is how the industry got its reputation; we don't do it.

  3. 03

    Client obligations sheet

    Before every treatment you sign a short sheet: what we'll do, what you must do (cover food, keep pets away, respect the interval), and what to do if anything seems wrong. Copies live in the logbook.

  4. 04

    Traceable chemistry

    Every product from a licensed importer, every purchase billed, every batch number logged on every visit. If anyone ever asks "what exactly did you use in my kitchen on March 14th" — we can answer in one minute.
