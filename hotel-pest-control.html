[R-02] Room Shield

Pest Management For
Hotels & Guesthouses

Room Shield is a quarterly inspection programme for hotels, guesthouses, lodges and hostels across the Kathmandu Valley. Every guest room is checked and gets its own record card. Rooms found with bed bugs are treated separately. Quoted per property.

Scheduled Room
Inspections

  • Every guest room inspected once a quarter: mattress seams, headboards, bed frames, furniture joints and skirting
  • A record card for each room, showing the inspection date and what was found
  • Corridors, storage, laundry and back-of-house areas included in inspections
  • Rodent programme for service areas and roof lines
  • Inspections scheduled around housekeeping and occupancy

An inspection tells you what is there on the day. It cannot stop a guest bringing bed bugs in, which is why rooms are checked every quarter.

When A Room
Needs Treatment

  1. 01

    Tell us

    Message us on WhatsApp. We reply within 2–3 hours, between 5 AM and 9 PM, and agree an inspection time.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    We inspect the room and the rooms around it to see how far the problem has spread.

  3. 03

    Treat

    Bed bugs are treated with steam plus a residual treatment, over three visits. The fee is per room and quoted before we start.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    We record the treatment on the room card and tell you when the room can be used again.

Hotel Pricing
Explained

Room Shield is quoted per property, based on how many guest rooms you have. Treatment of an infested room is a separate per-room fee. You get both numbers, and any minimum charge, in writing before you sign.

Sites outside Ring Road may carry a travel charge, which we tell you before booking.

Hotel
Questions

What hotel owners, general managers and housekeeping managers ask before they sign.

Is bed-bug treatment included in the inspection price?

No. The programme covers the quarterly inspections and the records. A room found with bed bugs is treated at a separate per-room fee, which we quote before we start.

Is there a minimum charge for a small guesthouse?

We quote each property on its room count. Any minimum charge is written into the quote before you sign.

Can rooms stay in use during inspection or treatment?

We schedule inspections around housekeeping and occupancy. A room being treated is taken out of use until we tell you it can be used again; how long depends on the treatment.

How quickly do you respond to a guest complaint?

We reply on WhatsApp within 2–3 hours, between 5 AM and 9 PM, and agree an inspection time with you. A reply is not a completed treatment.

Does the programme include the hotel kitchen and shared areas?

Corridors, storage, laundry and back-of-house areas are part of the inspections. A hotel kitchen is quoted separately under our restaurant programme.
