02 / Why It Matters

Who asks for this file

  1. 01

    DFTQC inspectors

    The Food Hygiene and Quality Act 2081 requires pest-control measures at every licensed food premises. A logbook with batch numbers ends that conversation in your favour. Read the guide →

  2. 02

    HACCP & hotel audits

    Any HACCP-aligned audit — and every serious hotel group audit — requires documented pest monitoring with trend data. This record is built to that standard.

  3. 03

    Corporate clients & tour operators

    Foreign tour operators and corporate caterers ask their venues for pest-control evidence. Your certificate and file are the answer.

  4. 04

    Food delivery platform audits

    Delivery platforms increasingly audit kitchen hygiene. The wall certificate and photo reports satisfy them without a second visit.
