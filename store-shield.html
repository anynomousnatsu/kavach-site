[SEC-01] The Programme

What Store Shield covers

  1. 01

    Rodent station network

    Tamper-proof bait stations, numbered, mapped to your floor plan, checked station-by-station every visit. Take is recorded and trended — you see the pressure moving before the damage happens.

  2. 02

    Stored-product-insect monitoring

    Pheromone traps for beetles and moths in grain, flour, pulses and packaged stock. Captures counted monthly. An SPI problem caught at trap level costs nothing; caught at customer level it costs the account.

  3. 03

    Monthly trend reporting

    Activity index per zone, month over month, with photos. When your buyer's auditor asks for pest-control evidence, you forward one PDF.

  4. 04

    Proofing recommendations

    Every gap, broken door sweep and open drain documented with a photo and a fixed-price proofing quote. Proofing is the only pest work that permanently cuts your risk — we push it because it works.
