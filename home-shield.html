01 / The Visits

What each quarterly visit includes

  1. 01

    Kitchen & bathroom treatment

    Gel baiting in harbourage points, crack-and-crevice residual, drain treatment. The same protocol as our restaurant work, sized for a home.

  2. 02

    Perimeter & entry points

    Balconies, door thresholds, utility shafts and meter boxes — where Kathmandu's mixed-use buildings feed pests into your flat.

  3. 03

    Seasonal cover

    Mosquito attention in monsoon (dengue season), rodent focus in winter, fly pressure in between. The schedule follows the pest calendar, not ours.

  4. 04

    The record

    Products used, batch numbers, re-entry intervals — texted to you after every visit. Yes, for a house. It's how we do everything.
