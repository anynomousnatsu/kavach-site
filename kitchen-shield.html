03 / Night Window

Nothing lost. Not one cover.

Every incumbent in Kathmandu publishes 8 AM–5 PM hours. A kitchen cannot be treated during service. Ours runs the other way around.

  1. 22:00Your kitchen closes. Last orders out, surfaces cleared.
  2. 22:30We arrive. Torch inspection, station checks, treatment begins.
  3. 01:30We finish. Logbook signed, photos taken, re-entry intervals noted.
  4. 06:00You open. Report is already on your phone. Nothing lost.

07 / Questions

Restaurant owners ask

Is it safe to cook the next morning?

Yes. Food-contact zones get gel baits and crack-and-crevice applications only — never broadcast sprays. Where a residual is applied, the re-entry interval is logged and always expires before your 6 AM open. Surfaces should be wiped as part of your normal morning clean.

What do you need from us before a night visit?

Access (a key holder or a staff member), food put away or covered, and sinks empty. We send a one-line reminder the evening of every scheduled visit.

How long is the contract?

Twelve months, first month in advance, monthly billing on the 1st. eSewa, Khalti, FonePay, bank transfer or cheque. VAT bill provided.

What if we see a cockroach a week after your visit?

You message us and we are back within 48 hours, free. Unlimited times. That callback guarantee is a clause in your contract, not a promise on a leaflet.

Do you treat the whole building or just our kitchen?

Your contract covers your premises. In Kathmandu's mixed-use buildings, pressure often comes from next door — our monitoring shows exactly where, and the report gives you the evidence to raise it with your landlord or neighbour.
