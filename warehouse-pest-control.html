[S-03] Store Shield

Pest Management For
Warehouses & Godowns

Store Shield is a monthly programme for dry warehouses, godowns and distribution stores across the Kathmandu Valley: a numbered bait-station network, stored-product insect monitoring and a written record of every visit. Quoted per site. We do not currently service cold stores, and we do not offer fumigation.

What Your Site
Programme Covers

  • A numbered, mapped bait-station network inside and around the building, checked every visit
  • Stored-product insect monitoring traps near the goods most at risk
  • Inspection of goods-in areas, racking and loading bays
  • A logbook entry and a WhatsApp photo report after every visit
  • Rodent-proofing recommendations, quoted separately

Planning Coverage
Around Your Operation

Floor area, layout, what you store and how goods come in decide how many stations and traps you need. We plan the network from your details and, where needed, a site visit, then number every station on a map. Visits are booked between 5 AM and 9 PM around your loading times.

Monitoring
Records

What your management team receives. A Kavach service record or certificate is not a government approval.

Station map

Every bait station numbered and marked, so each check can be traced.

Visit logbook

What we found, what we did and which products and batches we used.

Photo report

Sent on WhatsApp after each visit.

Structural Gaps &
Optional Work

Gaps under doors, damaged mesh and open drains let rodents in. We point these out on your record and can quote proofing work separately: steel mesh, door sweeps and gap sealing.

Warehouse
Questions

What warehouse managers and distributors ask before they sign.

What does a monthly warehouse pest management programme cost?

Store Shield is quoted per site, based on floor area, layout and what you store. Send us the details on WhatsApp and we reply within 2–3 hours, between 5 AM and 9 PM.

Can you cover cold rooms as well as dry storage?

Not at the moment. We service dry warehouses and godowns only.

Are rodent proofing and bait stations included?

The quote lists the bait stations and traps your site needs and what they cost. Proofing work such as mesh, door sweeps and gap sealing is quoted separately before we do it.

What monitoring records will our management team receive?

A numbered station map, a logbook entry for every visit and a photo report on WhatsApp. We do not currently produce monthly trend reports.

Do you provide fumigation or export treatment certificates?

No. We do not offer fumigation and cannot issue fumigation or phytosanitary certificates.
